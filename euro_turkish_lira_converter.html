Which pair should you read?

EUR/TRY means the price of euros in lira: €1 ≈ ₺55.21.

TRY/EUR means the price of lira in euros: ₺1 ≈ €0.0181.

Practical tip for Turkey 🇹🇷

When a card terminal or ATM asks whether to charge you in euros or lira, paying in TRY (lira) is often the better choice because the terminal’s euro conversion may be less favourable. Your bank’s own fees and rates still apply.

For estimates only. Always check the rate and fees offered by your bank or exchange office before changing money.
